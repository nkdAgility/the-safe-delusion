The guide invites Scaled Agile Inc. to engage with the community and to act on whatever part of the feedback is useful. That invitation stands, and this page makes it public.

## The offer

**To Scaled Agile Inc.** — a response to any part of the guide will be published here in full and unedited. No commentary will be attached to it. If it identifies a factual error, that will be raised with the curators through the guide's normal contribution process.

**To anyone named or quoted in the guide** — if you have been misquoted, or your position has moved, say so and it will be published here. The guide's own contribution guidelines already ask that anyone quoted is informed so they can confirm they still agree with the quote.

**To the organisations described in the case studies** — if the account of your organisation is incomplete or has been overtaken by events, that response will be published here too.

## How to respond

Post to the [Agile Informed Decision Making group](https://groups.google.com/g/agileinformeddecisionmaking), where the curators and the wider community discuss the guide directly. Introduce yourself and say what you would like to respond to.

Two things are worth knowing before you post, because the group software enforces both silently:

- **Post through the web interface, or email the group from the address you subscribed with.** A message sent from any other address is lost without a bounce.
- **Attachments are discarded.** Google Groups does not allow them, and it does not warn you. Paste the text or link to it.

## What will happen

Responses are published verbatim. Length is not a filter. Disagreement is not a filter.

Two things are: anything unlawful, and anything directed at an individual rather than at the argument. The guide asks for a civil conversation rather than a shouting match, and that standard applies here.
