This site exists to serve one document: *Information for decision-makers considering the SAFe framework*. The guide is the work. Everything else here is a summary of it and a way in.

## The guide

A collective effort assembled mainly by members of the Agile community, curated by **Luca Minudel** and **Yves Hanoulle**, with contributions from Thomas J. Owens, Chris Combe, Luis Azcona Latasa, Stefan Sonnenberg-Carstens, Martin Hinshelwood, Ove Holmberg, Steve Bement and many others.

It gathers case studies, expert assessments and practitioner accounts, with every source listed so any claim can be checked against its origin.

[Read the guide →](/safe-decision-makers/)

## How it is assembled

The case studies were selected on a stated standard: verified against multiple sources, or drawn from sources unlikely to hold a vested interest. That is why several widely-circulated adoption stories do not appear, and why three that do appear are ones the framework's own vendor published or showcased.

The guide is explicit about the limits of its own argument. No single case study, expert opinion or practitioner account settles anything on its own; the weight is in how consistently they agree. It is equally explicit that its argument is with the framework rather than with the people who teach it.

## Contributing

The guide is open and still being curated. Corrections, additions and disagreement all go through the same route.

- [Join the discussion group](https://groups.google.com/g/agileinformeddecisionmaking)
- [The contribution guidelines](/safe-decision-makers/#contributors-and-guidelines-for-contributions)
- [The repository](https://github.com/nkdAgility/the-safe-delusion)

If you believe you have been misquoted, or you want to respond to any part of the guide, there is a [standing right of reply](/right-of-reply/).

## This site

The pages here — the summary of what it cost, the guidance on getting out, the answers to common objections — are produced by this site, not by the guide's curators. They compress and point; they do not add evidence. Where a page and the guide differ, the guide is correct.

The guide is a versioned, edition-stamped document. These pages summarise the **2024.8** edition.
